## 🔗 Blockchain & Smart Contracts

AgriChain uses blockchain technology to provide transparent and verifiable agricultural transactions.

```text
Farmer
   ↓
List Product
   ↓
Fair Pricing
   ↓
Smart Contract
   ↓
Blockchain
   ↓
Consumer
   ↓
Product Verification
```

## 🎯 Problem We Solve

Agriculture faces challenges such as:

* Unfair pricing and dependence on intermediaries
* Lack of supply-chain transparency
* Limited access to market intelligence
* Food wastage and inefficient surplus distribution
* Limited access to agricultural funding

AgriChain addresses these challenges through **blockchain transparency, AI-driven insights, direct marketplace access, and decentralized community features**.

## 💡 Vision

Our goal is to create a more **transparent, fair, and sustainable agricultural ecosystem** where farmers have better market access and consumers can make informed purchasing decisions.
